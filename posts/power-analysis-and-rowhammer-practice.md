# Practice work: power traces on a ChipWhisperer Nano and Rowhammer in simulation

## ChipWhisperer Nano

During my IIT Bhilai internship in 2026 I worked through power-trace exploration on a ChipWhisperer Nano, in Jupyter notebooks, to see how power traces look and what they show. I did not capture power traces on TROPIC01, and that remains ahead of me.

The homepage has an interactive demo of the same idea, a simulated power-analysis attack where traces pile up and the correct key byte stands out from the wrong guesses.

## Rowhammer in simulation

I implemented a Rowhammer attack in a simulated environment, using university course labs and simulators. It is a simulation and not an attack on real DRAM.
