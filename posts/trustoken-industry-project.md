# TrusToken: security analysis of an industry crypto token

## What it is

TrusToken is a cryptographic USB token built by C-DAC Noida and IIT Bhilai. I worked on its security analysis from June to August 2026 at the de.ci.phe.red Lab, IIT Bhilai, under Dr. Dhiman Saha.

## What I did

An earlier B.Tech Honours thesis at the lab had already attacked the token. I replicated attacks from that thesis and documented new attack vectors and observations. I wrote the results into a consolidated report and a presentation, and delivered both to the lab. Dr. Saha invited me to keep working on it informally after the internship.

## What I do not publish

TrusToken is an industry project and the report cannot leave the lab. This note does not describe any finding, method or count, and I will not add them here. A paper is not planned for the same reason.
