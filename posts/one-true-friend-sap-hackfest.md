# One True Friend: an inclusive workforce platform for SAP Hackfest 2026

## The project

One True Friend is an Inclusive Workforce Intelligence platform. I led the team in SAP Hackfest North Region 2026. We reached Round 2, the top five, and then the finale round.

## The design

The pitch used six agents: Skill Discovery, Market Intelligence, Learning Pathway, Inclusive Matching, Employer Readiness, and Compliance and Bias Audit. A human reviews every match. The platform is free for workers and paid for by organisations, and the employer-facing case rests on compliance, legal and accessibility risk.

## The prototype

For Round 2 we built a working prototype in SAP Business Application Studio. The finale judged problem specificity, systems thinking, business viability and a working demo, and each team had one laptop and a backup demo video.

## Where it stands

The finale round is over. The repository is private.
