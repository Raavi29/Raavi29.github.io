# Saheli: a WhatsApp-first marketplace for homebound women

## The project

Saheli is a labour marketplace that lets homebound women take contracts from small businesses. I led a team of five at the CII Yi Yuva hackathon in Dehradun, and we reached the national grand finale in 2026.

## The design

- It works through WhatsApp, so a user needs no new app.
- A trust score gives businesses a reason to hand work to someone they have not met.
- Payouts go through UPI.
- Users can speak in Hindi instead of typing.
